Questions, answered.

I only have 30 minutes. Is that enough?

Yes. Most Ekam classes are designed to fit into 30 to 45 minutes, with no commute or setup beyond joining from home.

Do I need a wearable?

No. You can browse and book without one. Apple Health and Health Connect are optional and only help personalize suggestions.

Can the coach see me? Do I have to be on camera?

Your camera is your choice. Turning it on lets the instructor offer more useful form guidance during class.

What happens after my 14 free days?

The free period ends. Choose a membership if you want to keep booking — if you don’t, you’re never billed.

What if I miss a class I booked?

You can manage upcoming bookings from your account. The cancellation terms shown when you book apply to that class.
