Laura Moran

Certified Pilates Instructor Through Basi Pilates

Teaching since 2018, I am a certified Pilates instructor through BASI Pilates. While Pilates is my passion and focus, I am a 200-RYT and have my CPT through NASM. I create effective workouts that utilize the whole body, drawing on my different fitness educations. Proper form and setup are crucial to execution of the exercise, and I always try to setup the students for success. When I'm not teaching or working out myself, I am probably spending time with my furry children, reading, or listening or playing music.

Center your mind and fire up your deep core in just 30 minutes. This express mat Pilates flow focuses on controlled, low-impact movements designed to lengthen, sculpt, and build functional stability throughout the entire body. Perfect for all fitness levels, tune in to your breath, and leave feeling taller, stronger, and energized.

Class plan

30 min

  1. 4 min

  2. 22 min

    Core & sculpt

  3. 4 min

    Cool-down

    Stretch and down-regulate the breath.
